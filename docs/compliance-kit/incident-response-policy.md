# Incident Response Policy (Template)

## Severity Levels
- Sev1: Customer-impacting outage or confirmed data breach.
- Sev2: Major degraded functionality.
- Sev3: Limited impact or near-miss.

## Workflow
1. Detect and triage.
2. Declare incident channel and owner.
3. Contain, eradicate, recover.
4. Communicate status updates.
5. Postmortem within 5 business days.

## Minimum Artifacts
- Timeline with UTC timestamps.
- Root cause statement.
- Corrective/preventive actions with owners.
